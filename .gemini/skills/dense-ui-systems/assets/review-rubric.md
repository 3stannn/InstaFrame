# UI acceptance rubric
Mark each PASS, FAIL, NOT APPLICABLE, or NOT VERIFIED and attach evidence.

1. The primary work surface serves the domain task; no decorative card grid.
2. Neutral surfaces, hairline structure, at most one functional accent, and no prohibited gradients/glows.
3. Compact hierarchy without illegible type or undersized interactive targets.
4. Real labels and clearly marked synthetic fixtures; no fake live metrics.
5. Native semantics, meaningful labels, keyboard access, visible focus, and non-color status cues.
6. Loading, empty-new, empty-filtered, error, forbidden, pending, and success behavior.
7. Narrow screen, 200% zoom, overflow, and long-text/large-number cases.
8. Contrast and reduced-motion behavior verified with appropriate tools.
9. No repeated nested cards, meaningless icons, or oversized diffuse shadows.
10. Screenshots/browser checks actually performed, or explicitly unverified.
